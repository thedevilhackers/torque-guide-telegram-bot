# Automatic replies for customers

These messages reply to customers automatically, invite them to the workshop and ask for their vehicle details. You switch them on yourself in the WhatsApp Business app and in Meta Business Suite (for Instagram and Facebook). It's free and takes about 10 minutes.

Every message uses the same details:

- **Workshop:** L-12, Argora Housing Colony, Ranchi, Jharkhand 834002
- **Map link:** https://maps.google.com/?q=L-12+Argora+Housing+Colony+Ranchi+834002
- **Hours:** Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 6 pm
- **WhatsApp:** +91 87096 47229

If you have your own Google Maps listing, use its share link instead of the map link above.

## 1. WhatsApp greeting message

The greeting message goes automatically to anyone who messages you for the first time, or after 14 days of no messages.

**To set it up:**

1. Open the **WhatsApp Business** app.
2. Go to **Settings → Business tools → Greeting message** and turn it on.
3. Paste one of the messages below.
4. Under **Recipients**, choose "Everyone", then **Save**.

If your number is on the normal WhatsApp app, install WhatsApp Business first and register the same number. Your chats move across.

## 2. WhatsApp away message

The away message replies when the workshop is closed.

**To set it up:**

1. Go to **Settings → Business tools → Away message** and turn it on.
2. Under **Schedule**, choose **Outside of business hours**.
3. Set your business hours (Mon–Sat, 9–6) in **Business profile → Hours**.
4. Paste the message below, then **Save**.

```
Message ke liye shukriya! 🙏 Workshop abhi band hai (Mon–Sat, 9 am – 6 pm).

Apni car ka model, year aur Petrol/Diesel bhej dijiye. Hum agle din subah sabse pehle reply karenge.
📍 L-12, Argora Housing Colony, Ranchi
```

## 3. WhatsApp quick replies

Quick replies are ready answers that you send by typing **/** and a shortcut, such as `/visit`.

**To set them up:**

1. Go to **Settings → Business tools → Quick replies**.
2. Tap **+**, paste a message, type its shortcut, then **Save**.
3. Repeat for each message below.

**`/visit`**: invite the customer to the workshop.
```
Aap gaadi lekar workshop aa jaiye, hum health check karke sahi stage aur estimate batayenge. 🔧
📍 L-12, Argora Housing Colony, Ranchi 834002
🗺️ https://maps.google.com/?q=L-12+Argora+Housing+Colony+Ranchi+834002
🕘 Mon–Sat, 9 am – 6 pm
Kis din aur kis time aana chahenge? Hum slot book kar dete hain.
```

**`/car`**: ask for the vehicle details.
```
Sahi estimate ke liye ye details bhejiye:
1️⃣ Car model aur variant
2️⃣ Saal (year)
3️⃣ Petrol ya Diesel
4️⃣ Manual ya Automatic
5️⃣ Kitne km chali hai
6️⃣ Koi modification pehle se hai?
```

**`/price`**: answer "how much does it cost?".
```
Price aapki car aur stage par depend karta hai. 🙂
Apni car ka model, year aur fuel bhejiye, hum exact estimate bhej denge.
Stage 1 (sirf software) aam taur par ek din mein ho jaata hai.
Apni car ke estimated power figures yahan dekhiye: unityperformance.co
```

**`/stage1`**: explain Stage 1.
```
Stage 1 = sirf ECU software tuning, koi part change nahi. ⚡
✅ Pehle health check
✅ Aapki original file save rehti hai, jab chahein stock par wapas
✅ Emission parts lage rehte hain
Turbo petrol mein aam taur par ~20% aur turbo diesel mein ~15–20% zyada power (estimated, healthy standard cars).
```

**`/booked`**: confirm an appointment. Fill in the blanks before you send it.
```
Aapka slot book ho gaya ✅
📅 Date: ____
🕘 Time: ____
🚗 Car: ____
📍 L-12, Argora Housing Colony, Ranchi
🗺️ https://maps.google.com/?q=L-12+Argora+Housing+Colony+Ranchi+834002
Koi badlaav ho to isi number par bata dijiye. Milte hain! 🏁
```

**`/thanks`**: send after the job.
```
Unity Performance chunne ke liye shukriya! 🙏
Tune ke baad pehle 10 minute gaadi aaram se chalayein jab tak oil garam na ho, aur jo fuel tune ke liye bana hai wahi daalein.
Koi bhi sawaal ho to isi number par message kijiye.
```

## 4. Instagram and Facebook automatic replies

**To set them up:**

1. Open **Meta Business Suite**, on the app or at business.facebook.com.
2. Go to **Inbox → Automations** (on some versions: **Inbox → ⚙ → Automated responses**).
3. Set up the three automations below.

### Instant reply

Turn on **Instant reply** for both Instagram and Facebook, and paste:
```
Hi! 👋 Unity Performance, Ranchi se sampark karne ke liye shukriya.
Apni car ka model, year aur Petrol/Diesel yahin bhejiye, ya WhatsApp kijiye: wa.me/918709647229
Workshop: L-12, Argora Housing Colony, Ranchi (Mon–Sat, 9 am – 6 pm). Aane se pehle message karein, hum time book kar denge. 🏁
```

### Away message

Turn on the **Away message** for outside business hours. Use the same text as the WhatsApp away message above.

### FAQ buttons

Add up to four **frequently asked questions**. Customers tap them and get the answer instantly.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Meri car ka estimate? | Apni car ka model, year aur Petrol/Diesel bhejiye, hum estimate bhej denge. Ya apni car yahan search karein: unityperformance.co |
| Workshop kahan hai? | L-12, Argora Housing Colony, Ranchi 834002. Map: https://maps.google.com/?q=L-12+Argora+Housing+Colony+Ranchi+834002 · Mon–Sat, 9 am – 6 pm |
| Kya tuning safe hai? | Haan. Har tune se pehle health check, aapki original file save, emission parts lage rehte hain, aur har tune road par log karke check hota hai. |
| Kitna time lagta hai? | Stage 1 aam taur par ek din. Stage 2 ek se do din. Stage 3 parts par depend karta hai. |

## Want a fully automatic WhatsApp assistant later?

The setup above uses only the free WhatsApp Business app. A bot that holds a whole conversation needs more:

- The **WhatsApp Business Platform** (the API).
- **Meta business verification**.
- A small server connected to your website.

Such a bot could ask the questions one by one, look up the car's power figures from your website and book a slot automatically. It's possible to add to your existing app, but your number's WhatsApp setup would change. Start with the greeting message and quick replies above; they cover most of it at no cost.
