# How to post and boost the ads

Before you start, check two things:

- **Instagram is a Professional account.** In the Instagram app: Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account → Business.
- **Instagram is linked to your Facebook Page.** At business.facebook.com: Settings → Accounts → Instagram accounts → Connect.

Then you can post to both from one place, and schedule everything in one sitting.

---

## Part 1: Schedule all the posts at once (about 15 minutes, on a laptop)

1. Open **business.facebook.com** and choose your Unity Performance Page.
2. Click **Create post**. Tick both **Facebook** and **Instagram**.
3. **Add photo/video**: choose the post image (the `…-post-1080x1350.png` file).
4. Paste the matching caption from `CAPTIONS.md` (English) or `CAPTIONS-HINDI.md` (Hindi).
5. Click the arrow next to **Publish** → **Schedule** → choose the date and time from the plan below → **Schedule**.
6. For a video: **Create reel** instead, upload the `…-reel-1080x1920.mp4`, add music from the library, paste the caption and schedule it.
7. For Stories: **Create story**, upload the matching `…-story-1080x1920.png` and schedule it for the same day.
8. Repeat for each day. Everything then appears under **Planner**, where you can drag posts to other days.

The best time to post in Ranchi is usually **7 to 9 pm**, or **11 am to 1 pm** at weekends.

Also put each day's Story image on your **WhatsApp Status**. Your existing customers see it for free.

## Part 2: Boost (paid ads)

Boost a post once it has been live for a few hours and has some likes. Open the post in the Instagram app and tap **Boost post**. You can also click **Boost** under the post in Business Suite.

| Setting | Boost A: Stage 1 video | Boost B: Offers | Boost C: Franchise | Boost D: Mechanics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | More messages → WhatsApp | More messages → WhatsApp | More messages → WhatsApp (or website visits to /franchise) | More website visits → unityperformance.co/mechanics |
| **Location** | Ranchi + 40 km | Ranchi + 25 km | Jharkhand, Bihar, West Bengal, Odisha | Ranchi + 25 km |
| **Age** | 21–45 | 21–55 | 25–55 | 18–45 |
| **Interests** | Cars, Automobiles, Car tuning, Motorsport, SUVs | Cars, Car wash, Automobiles | Entrepreneurship, Small business, Franchising, Automobiles | Auto mechanic, Automobile repair shop, Cars |
| **Budget** | ₹300/day × 7 days | ₹250/day × 7 days | ₹300/day × 7 days | ₹150/day × 5 days |

That comes to about **₹6,700 plus GST** over two weeks.

**Settings that matter:**
- **"More messages → WhatsApp"** puts a WhatsApp button on the ad, so people land straight in your chat. Use the number **+91 87096 47229**, linked under Settings → WhatsApp accounts.
- **Payment**: add UPI or a card under Settings → Payments. Meta adds GST to ad spend in India, and you can enter your GSTIN there to claim it.

**After 3 days**, check **Insights** on each boost. Keep the ads that bring the most WhatsApp messages and stop the others. Then put the money from the stopped ads into the winner.

**If an ad is rejected**, it's usually for the wording "earn money". Change the Refer & earn caption to "Get rewards for referring friends" and try again.

## Why Claude didn't post these for you

Posting and boosting need you to be logged in to your own Instagram and Facebook accounts, and boosting charges your card. This cloud session has no access to your phone or browser. Never share your password in a chat.

If you use **Claude in Chrome** on your own computer, it can work through Business Suite alongside you while you stay logged in. You would still approve each post and the payment yourself.
