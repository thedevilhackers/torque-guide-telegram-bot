What we do

Services

From a single remap to a full build, every job is planned around the car you drive and the fuel you run.

01

ECU Remapping

Stage 1, Stage 1+ ethanol and custom maps for petrol and diesel. We read your ECU with Autotuner or KESS3 and save the original file before anything changes.

  • Stage 1
  • Stage 1+
  • E20–E85
02

Data-Logged Tuning

We log boost, timing, fuel trims and temperatures on real road runs, then refine the map until every reading is clean. Proof on the road, not on a poster.

  • Live logging
  • Road tested
03

Exhaust & Intake

Performance exhausts, intakes and intercoolers chosen to suit your map, so the hardware and the software work together instead of against each other.

  • Exhaust
  • Intake
  • Intercooler
04

Turbo Kits

Hybrid and bigger-turbo upgrades with the fuel and cooling they need, mapped as one package so the power comes in smooth and stays reliable.

  • Hybrid turbo
  • Supporting mods
05

Suspension

Coilovers, lowering springs and alignment set up for sharper turn-in and better grip, without ruining the ride on everyday roads.

  • Coilovers
  • Springs
  • Alignment
06

Wraps & Protection

Gloss, satin and matte colour wraps, stripes and ceramic coating to change the look and keep the paint underneath protected.

  • Colour wraps
  • Stripes
  • Ceramic
