unity-car.glb

Based on "Car Concept" from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets:
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept
That asset was made from a public-domain (CC0) model by Unity Fan on Sketchfab.

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: the Khronos and 3D Commerce logos (which the licence excludes), the windscreen wipers
and the alternative paint colours were removed, the meshes were simplified and the textures
compressed (see landing/prepare-car.mjs). On the home page the glass, paint and number plate
are restyled.
