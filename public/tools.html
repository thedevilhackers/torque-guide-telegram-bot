How we read it

Three ways in.

Your original software is backed up first, whichever way we connect.

  • OBD

    Diagnostic port

    Read and written through the car's OBD socket. Nothing is removed from the car.

  • Bench

    On the bench

    The ECU is taken out and connected on the bench. Newer locked ECUs need this, often only once.

  • Boot

    Boot mode

    The ECU is opened and read through its boot connection, for ECUs that can't be read any other way.
