From Shillong

How it works.

Hill roads call for low and mid-range torque, which is where a Stage 1 map adds most of its gain.

  • 1. Find your car

    Search your car to see its ECU and the expected Stage 1, 2 and 3 gains.

  • 2. Message us

    Send the car's details on WhatsApp. We check the ECU and tell you the price before you travel.

  • 3. Visit Ranchi

    Bring the car to our workshop in Argora, Ranchi, at the time we book together.

  • 4. Drive home

    Your original file is saved, so the car can go back to standard whenever you need.

We keep all emissions equipment (DPF, EGR, AdBlue, catalysts) and don't remove immobilisers.
